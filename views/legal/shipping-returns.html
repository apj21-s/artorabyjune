Shipping, returns & refunds

Last updated: {{year}}

Shipping

  • We ship across India with tracked courier partners.
  • Ready-made totes are dispatched in 2–3 working days. Commissions ship once painted, sealed and the balance is paid.
  • Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your pincode.
  • Tracking details are emailed as soon as your parcel ships.

Returns — ready-made totes

If you are not happy with a ready-made tote, contact us within 7 days of delivery. Unused items in original packaging can be returned for a refund minus shipping costs.

Commissions

Custom pieces are made just for you, so they can’t be returned for a change of mind. If your piece arrives damaged or differs materially from the sketch you approved, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we will repair, repaint or refund.

Deposits

If you cancel a commission before painting begins, your deposit is refunded in full. Once painting has started, the deposit is non-refundable.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Damaged parcels

Please record a short video while opening your parcel. If anything is damaged, send it to us within 48 hours.
