Privacy policy

Last updated: {{year}}

Artora by June (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, the story you share and any reference photos you upload.
  • Account details: your email, name and a securely hashed password, or your Google profile if you sign in with Google.
  • Payments: handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI credentials; we only receive a payment confirmation.
  • Messages and newsletter: what you send us, and your email if you subscribe.

How we use it

Only to make, ship and support your order, to reply to you, and — if you opted in — to send occasional studio letters. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the studio: our payment processor (Razorpay), email provider (Resend), image storage (Cloudinary), hosting provider, and courier partners for delivery.

Reference photos

Photos you send are used only to paint your piece. We will never post your photos or finished piece publicly without your permission.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to the studio. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and store your bag in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies.

Contact

Questions? Write to us.
